# Introduction to Mexican cuisine
Mexican cuisine is a vibrant, ancient blend of Indigenous Mesoamerican and Spanish culinary traditions. Renowned for bold flavors, it relies on staple crops like corn, beans, and chili peppers , and is celebrated globally as an official UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity

## Core Foundations
- Maize (Corn): The undisputed king of Mexican food. It is ground into masa to create the ubiquitous tortillas, tamales, and tostadas.
- Chilis: Providing both heat and depth, chilies are a cornerstone . Common varieties include jalapeño, serrano, and dried chipotle or guajillo.
- Fresh Produce & Spices: Native ingredients like avocados (used in guacamole), tomatoes, cilantro, cacao, and vanilla define the fresh profile of the food.
- Spanish Influence: The 16th century brought European cooking techniques (such as frying) and new ingredients like pork, beef, cheese, and wheat.
