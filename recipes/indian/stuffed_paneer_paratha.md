# Stuffed Paneer Paratha

- Cuisine Type: Indian
- Preparation Time: 30 to 40 minutes
- Cooking Time: 15 to 20 minutes
- Serving Size: 8 parathas

## Ingredients (For the Dough)

- Whole Wheat Flour (Atta), 2 1/2 cups
- Warm Water, 3/4 to 1 cup
- Salt, Large pinch
- Oil, 1 Teaspoon

## Ingredients (For the Filling)
- Paneer, 200 to 250g
- Frozen Green Garlic, 2/3 cup OR Scallions, a bunch and Garlic, 4 cloves
- Corriander, a handful
- Mint, a small bunch
- Green chilies, 8–12 OR to taste
- Salt, 1 teaspoon OR to taste
- Cumin powder, 1 teaspoon
- Black pepper, 1 teaspoon
- Garam masala, 2 teaspoon
- Coriander powder, 1 teaspoon
- White onion, 1/2

## Method

1. Add the flour to a bowl with 1 tsp oil and a big pinch of salt.
2. Mix, then pour in the warm water a little at a time, mixing as you go, until it comes together into a dough.
3. Knead for 2–3 minutes. It should not be too wet or sticky.
4. Cover the dough with a damp paper towel and let it rest for at least 20–30 minutes.
5. Add the green chilies and green garlic (or scallions + garlic) to a blender and pulse a few times so it’s still chunky and not fully a paste.
6. In a bowl, combine the shredded paneer with the chili–green garlic mixture you just made, corriander, mint, salt, cumin powder, black pepper, onion, garam masala, and coriander powder.
7. Mix well, taste and adjust it to taste.
8. Divide the dough into balls.
9. Keep a small bowl of dry flour nearby for dusting, roll each portion into a smooth ball, and return them to the bowl, covered, so they don’t dry out.
10. Dust your surface with flour to prevent sticking.
11. Flatten a ball, coat both sides in flour, and roll it out — go forward and back a few times, rotate 90°, and roll again, until you have a circle about 6 inches wide.
12. Place a large spoonful of filling in the center.
13. Bring the edges of the circle up and over the filling, gathering them at the top, and pinch them together so the filling is fully sealed inside so no filling peeking out.
14. Pinch off any excess dough at the top.
15. Flatten the stuffed ball gently, dust both sides with flour, and press with your palm to spread the filling evenly.
16. Carefully roll the stuffed disc back out into a 5–6 inch circle (or larger, if you like thinner parathas).
17. Roll gently and evenly so the filling doesn’t burst through. This is the key to a paneer paratha not breaking.
18. Heat a pan until hot, then lay the paratha on it.
19. Cook for 2–3 minutes, drizzle a little oil or ghee on top, then flip.
20. Cook the other side for 2–3 minutes, add a touch more ghee or oil, and cook until you see light golden-brown spots on both sides.




## Serving Suggestions: Eat as is or with curry.
