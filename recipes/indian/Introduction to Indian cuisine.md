# Introduction to Indian cuisine
Indian cuisine is a vast, diverse culinary landscape defined by its brilliant use of aromatic spices, diverse regional ingredients, and centuries-old traditions. Far from a single "curry," it features countless specialties—from creamy, dairy-rich North Indian meals to coconut-heavy, seafood-driven South Indian dishes.

## Major Regional Variations
- North India: Known for rich, thick gravies made with tomatoes, yogurt, or cream . Meals are frequently paired with leavened and unleavened flatbreads like naan and roti.
- South India: Features a tropical climate where rice, seafood, and coconut are staple ingredients. Dishes like dosa (savory rice and lentil pancakes) are incredibly popular.
- East & West India: Coastal regions like Goa incorporate strong Portuguese influences (like the spicy, vinegar-based vindaloo), while the East is famous for fish and sweet milk-based desserts .
