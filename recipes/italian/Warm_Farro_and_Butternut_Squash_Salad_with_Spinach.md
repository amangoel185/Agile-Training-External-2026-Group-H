# Warm Farro and Butternut Squash Salad with Spinach

- Cuisine Type: Italian
- Preparation Time: 5 - 10 minutes
- Cooking Time: 15 - 30 minutes
- Serving Size: 1 to 2 People

## Ingredients (clear list with quantities)

- Dried Farro, 1 1/2 cups
- Butternut Squash, 1 small
- Olive oil, 1 Tablespoon (approx.)
- Salt & Pepper, To taste
- Chevre Cheese OR Goat's Cheese, 4oz
- Fresh Spinach, 2 to 3 Cups (packed)
- Dried Thyme, 1/2 Teaspoon OR Fresh Thyme, 1 Teaspoon
- Dried Oregano, 1/2 Teaspoon OR Fresh Oregano, 1 Teaspoon

## Method (step-by-step instructions)

1. Cut the butternut squash into 3/4 inch cubes.
2. Toss the butternut squash with the olive, dried thyme, and a bit of salt.
3. Bake, stirring occasionally, in a preheated 205 °C degree oven.
4. Bake for 15 to 30 minutes or until tender.
5. Cook your farro according to the package directions or your desired tenderness.
6. Drain and return to pan.
7. Add the fresh spinach and just a bit of olive oil, cooking on low until spinach is lightly wilted.
8. Add the cooked butternut squash and warm everything through evenly.
9. Stir lightly so you don’t break up the squash.
10. Taste and add additional salt, pepper and olive if desired.


## Serving Suggestions:
- Once warmed, place in a large serving dish.
- Crumble cheese on top as well as fresh herbs like additional thyme, oregano or parsley.
