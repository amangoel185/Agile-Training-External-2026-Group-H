## Simple Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
**Italian Recipe 2**  

- Cuisine Type: Italian  
- Preparation Time: 5 min  
- Cooking Time: 15 min  
- Serving Size: 2  

### Ingredients (clear list with quantities)  
- Spaghetti, 200 g  
- Garlic, 3 cloves (sliced)  
- Olive oil, 2 tbsp  
- Chilli flakes, 1 tsp  
- Parsley, small handful (chopped)  
- Salt, to taste  

### Method (step-by-step instructions)  
- Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente  
- Heat olive oil and gently fry garlic until light golden  
- Add chilli flakes and cooked pasta, mix well  
- Toss with parsley and season with salt  

### Serving Suggestions  
Serve immediately with extra olive oil and grated cheese (optional).
