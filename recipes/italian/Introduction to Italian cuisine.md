# Introduction to Italian cuisine
Italian cuisine is a globally renowned Mediterranean style of cooking built on simplicity, high-quality seasonal produce, and minimal processing. It relies on a few key pillars—like olive oil, garlic, fresh herbs, pasta, and ripe tomatoes—to create bold, satisfying flavors.

## Regional Diversity
Italian food is the sum of its distinct regions rather than a single unified menu:
- The North: Dishes often feature butter, rice (risotto), polenta, and hearty meat
- The Center: Known as the country's breadbasket, this area focuses on farro, olive oil, and rustic stews (e.g., in Tuscany).
- The South: Celebrated for sun-drenched ingredients like tomatoes, fresh seafood, and citrus fruits . Naples is famously the birthplace of pizza.
