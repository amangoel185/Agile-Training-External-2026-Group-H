# 🍣 Japanese Recipe 2: Teriyaki Chicken (照り焼きチキン)

## 🧾 Ingredients (Serves 2)

- 2 chicken thighs (boneless, skin-on)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin (sweet rice wine)
- 1 tbsp sake (or dry white wine)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp grated ginger (optional)
- 1 clove garlic (optional, minced)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

---

## 🔪 Preparation

1. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels.
2. Lightly score the skin to help it cook evenly.

---

## 🍳 Cooking Instructions

1. Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
2. Place chicken skin-side down and cook for 5–6 minutes until crispy.
3. Flip and cook the other side for 3–4 minutes.
4. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar.
5. Pour the sauce into the pan.
6. Reduce heat and simmer, spooning sauce over the chicken.
7. Cook until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken (about 3–5 minutes).

---

## 🍽️ Serving Suggestions

- Slice and serve over steamed rice.
- Garnish with sesame seeds or spring onions.
- Add steamed vegetables for a balanced meal.

---

## ✅ Tips

- Don’t overcrowd the pan—cook in batches if needed.
- Use chicken thighs for best flavor and tenderness.
- Adjust sugar to taste depending on sweetness preference.
